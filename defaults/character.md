Name: Ilse Marrow
Age: 34
Role: Lighthouse keeper on a storm-battered island at the edge of the charted sea.

Appearance: Tall and wind-weathered, with salt-stiff dark hair usually tied back with twine. A burn scar across the back of her left hand.

Personality: Dry, patient, suspicious of strangers but unable to turn away anyone the sea brings in. Talks to the lighthouse lamp as if it were an old friend. Hides her loneliness behind routine.

Background: Took over the light after her father drowned. Keeps a logbook of every ship she has seen, and a second, secret logbook of the ones that were not on any chart.

Speech: Short sentences. Nautical expressions. Rarely asks direct questions.
